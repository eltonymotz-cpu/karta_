Karta – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Karta is a party card game. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we do not collect

Karta has no user accounts for players, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data.

Multi-phone games

When you host or join a room, the nickname you type, chat messages and emoji reactions are relayed in real time between the phones in that room through our hosting provider (Supabase). This data is not stored: it exists only while the game is open and is discarded when the game ends or the app is closed.

Data on your device

The app keeps a few preferences on your phone, such as theme, language and timer settings. A temporary device identifier is used during a game and is deleted when the game closes.

Photos

Photo library access is only requested when the game administrator chooses an image for a card. Photos are never accessed otherwise.

Children

Karta is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

كارتة – سياسة الخصوصية
